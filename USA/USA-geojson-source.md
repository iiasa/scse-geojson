## States of the USA

The [geoBoundaries-USA-ADM1_simplified.geojson](geoBoundaries-USA-ADM1_simplified.geojson) file is sourced from 
the [geoBoundaries](https://www.geoboundaries.org) website. 
The data is derived from the United States Census Bureau, MAF/TIGER Database (2018), 
and represents ADM1 (first-level administrative division) boundaries.

The GeoJSON is created for the hierarchy "States of the USA" as defined in 
[US_states.yaml](https://github.com/IAMconsortium/common-definitions/blob/main/definitions/region/US_states.yaml) 
in the common definitions repository.
